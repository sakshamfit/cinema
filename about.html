ABOUT · STATION ROAD · MADHUBAN, BIHAR

A hall, not a chain.
One screen, one town.

Arush Cineplex is a single-auditorium cinema on Station Road in Madhuban. One film at a time, three shows a day, and nobody in the room who is not watching it. This page is the short version of the place: what it is, what it has, and what it does not do.

01THE ROOM

Six hundred and twenty seats, two decks, one curtain.

The stall runs flat-backed and the balcony hangs over the last six rows of it. There is no bad seat here, only seats that are further from the lamp.

02THE WEEK

Twenty-one shows, and one film across all of them.

The film changes on Monday, with the marquee. Between Mondays there is nothing to compare it with, which is how most of India ran cinemas for fifty years.

03THE PEOPLE

Four of us, and one of us is the projector.

The counter, the aisle, the sound desk and the booth. The same hands check the level at 9:15 AM and take your money at 6:40 PM, so a complaint finds somebody who can answer it.

02 · THE LIMITSSAID PLAINLY, BEFORE YOU COME

What it is not.

A small hall is a set of choices. These are ours, written down so nobody has to be disappointed at the door. Everything on this page is provisional until the counter says otherwise — the counter always wins.

—NOT ON OFFER
  • No online booking, no app, no seat map. The window is the counter and the counter is the window.
  • No 3D, no recliners — one flat screen and seats that have been cleaned this week.
  • No second hall to move you to when the house is full. Wait for the next show or take the balcony.
  • No food from outside, no drinks in the stall. The counter has packets, water and paper cups.

ONE SCREENAC

Arush Cineplex,
in brief.

One auditorium on Station Road in Madhuban, Bihar. 620 seats across two decks, one 4K laser projector, one film at a time — three shows a day, a twelve minute interval, and a counter that takes cash from 9:00 AM. It is not a chain and it does not pretend to be: the person who checks the sound before the first show is often the person who sells your ticket.
