Good to Know

Questions? Answered.

How do I book tickets online?

Choose your date above, tap any show time on a film, pick your seats on the seat map, fill in your name and mobile number, and confirm. You'll get a booking ID — just show it at the counter or gate to collect your entry.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes. Call the box office at +91 98765 43210 at least 2 hours before show time with your booking ID for a full refund or a change to another show.

Do you show Bhojpuri films?

Absolutely! Madhuban loves Bhojpuri cinema, and so do we. We screen the latest Bhojpuri releases alongside Hindi films every week — check the Bhojpuri filter above.

Is outside food allowed inside?

Outside food isn't allowed in the auditorium, but our Interval Point counter serves fresh, affordable snacks — from samosas and chowmein to popcorn combos.

Is parking available?

Yes, free parking for cycles and two-wheelers is available within the premises, with a guard on duty during all shows.
