01 · THE WALKMADHUBAN · BIHAR 848202

You will hear it
before you see it.

Arush Cineplex sits on Station Road in Madhuban, opposite the bus stand, four hundred metres from the railway station. Auto-rickshaws from the platform will quote you twenty rupees for a three-minute drive and that is roughly right. There is no sign pointing from the station; there has never needed one.

    01THE QUEUE

    Two lines, one window.

    The left line is tickets, the right is the ticket you already paid for at the group desk. Show your card here if you want the student rate, not after the usher asks.

    02LATECOMERS

    We hold you until the scene allows it.

    Not to be difficult — the aisle light falls on the cloth. You will be let in at the next wide shot and the usher will find you a seat, not your seat.

    03WITH CHILDREN

    U/A, and the 10:00 AM house is theirs.

    Children may attend with an adult. There are no booster seats; the stall rows are low enough that it has never come up. On a Tuesday the morning show is the cheapest ticket of the week.

    ARUSH CINEPLEX · MADHUBAN, BIHAR

    ONE SCREENAC

    Arush Cineplex,
    in brief.

    One auditorium on Station Road in Madhuban, Bihar. 620 seats across two decks, one 4K laser projector, one film at a time — three shows a day, a twelve minute interval, and a counter that takes cash from 9:00 AM. It is not a chain and it does not pretend to be: the person who checks the sound before the first show is often the person who sells your ticket.
